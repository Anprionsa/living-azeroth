What this is

Each entry is one community issue from the Forever beta. It records what players are saying, what Blizzard has said or done, and the form of the feedback that fits Blizzard's own stated goals.

The first entries were drawn from the working notes "WoW Forever Beta: Day One Feedback". Later findings go into each issue's log. This page is a dated snapshot of the working tracker; the date sits at the top right.

Status

Fit with stated intent

Compatible
Asks Blizzard to hit a target it has already stated. The strongest kind of feedback.
Reversal
Asks Blizzard to abandon a decision it has defended. Worth registering once, unlikely to move on beta feedback alone.
Matches intent
Blizzard's explanation already covers it.
Bug
Not a design question. A straight report.
Relayed
Came secondhand from Reddit, X, or an image and has not been read at the source. Confirm before quoting it back at Blizzard.
