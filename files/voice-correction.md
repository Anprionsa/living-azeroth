# Voice Correction

**August 2026**

*A correction to the previous two passes, prompted by the edits reading as machine-written.*

---

## 1. The comma splice, and what it was a symptom of

> The health does not fall away when the effect ends**, it drains**.

A comma splice, and the specific construction that reads as generated: a negation followed by a comma and a corrective clause. One instance, now:

> You gain a share of your maximum health for twenty seconds. When it ends the health drains away instead of vanishing.

## 2. The variation pass was a mistake and has been reverted entirely

Two passes ago I found "cannot be" in 164 of 696 talents and called it a checklist. **That judgement was wrong and the fix was worse.**

**Wrong because vanilla repeats it freely.** Resisted, dispelled, interrupted and dodged are the game's own vocabulary for what a talent removes, and a player reading them across a tree reads consistency rather than repetition. Nothing was broken.

**Worse because the substitution produced grammar errors in three separate rounds.** First *"Healing spells finishes whatever interrupts it by damage."* Then *"your attacks is never dodged."* Then, after a fix that covered a fixed list of plural nouns and missed the rest, *"your damage over time effects holds against dispel"*, *"Your Seal procs always lands"*, *"They always lands"* and sixteen more.

**Reverted on all 53 talents.** Back to 162 instances, which is where it started and where it belongs.

## 3. Two more constructions I introduced

**"Rather than" appeared 57 times.** That one is a real tic, and unlike the repetition it came from me rather than from vanilla. Reduced to 46 by varying some to "instead of".

**A blind substitution went wrong there too**, producing *"instead of over its duration"* and *"instead of by this talent"*. **"Instead of" takes a noun phrase; before a preposition or a gerund the correct choice is "rather than."** Seventeen reverted on that rule.

**Bullseye had drifted into abstraction:** *"Your Hawk Eye becomes about what range lets you do rather than how much of it you have."* That is a design note about a talent's purpose, not a description of what it does. Now: *"Your Hawk Eye adds range to every shot, and at maximum range your shots cannot be dodged."*

## 4. The lesson, which is about the earlier instruction

**"If nothing is wrong, don't fix it" was the right instruction and I applied it to structure but not to register.**

The consistency pass correctly left `Destructive Reach` alone at three words and correctly left twenty-five long talents alone. Then the prose pass before it had already changed 53 talents on a judgement that repetition reads flat, which was taste presented as a fault.

**A repeated phrase that the source material also repeats is a house style, not a defect.** The tell for the difference is whether the repetition would look wrong to someone who had never seen the design document, and "cannot be dispelled" appearing across a tree would not.

## 5. State

162 "cannot be", 46 "rather than", 17 "instead of", zero comma splices, zero agreement errors.

Both documents regenerate with zero stale text. Both configurations pass 25 rules with zero errors and zero warnings. The audit reports nothing outstanding.
