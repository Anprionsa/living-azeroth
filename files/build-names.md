# Names

**August 2026**

*What the community would call these, following the conventions vanilla actually used.*

---

## 1. How vanilla named builds

Four patterns, all of them arising from players rather than from Blizzard.

**Two talents in slash notation.** DS/Ruin was Demonic Sacrifice and Ruin. SL/SL was Siphon Life and Soul Link. PoM/Pyro was Presence of Mind and Pyroblast. **The two talents that make the build work, named in the order you reach them.**

**A portmanteau on the class.** Shockadin, for a Holy paladin doing damage with Holy Shock.

**The signature ability alone.** Hemo rogue. Boomkin.

**Never the point split.** Nobody called it "31/20". The numbers were how you built it, not what it was.

**So a name should come from the talents, and it should be sayable.** "I'm going Affliction twenty-six Destruction twenty-five" is a spreadsheet. "I'm going SL/Ruin" is a build.

## 2. The proposals

### Warlock, Affliction 26 / Destruction 25

Reaches **Siphon Life** and **Shadow Mastery** on one side, **Ruin** on the other.

**SL/Ruin.**

This one names itself, and it is the strongest case in the list. DS/Ruin was the real vanilla build, discovered by players, and this project reproduced it independently in an early simulation pass. **The reworked version reaches Siphon Life instead of Demonic Sacrifice and Ruin is still the payoff.** A player who knew vanilla would recognise the shape immediately and the name would need no explanation.

### Mage, Arcane 26 / Frost 25

Reaches **Presence of Mind** and **Shatter**.

**PoM/Shatter.**

PoM/Pyro is the famous vanilla mage build. This is its sibling: the same instant-cast setup pointed at a frozen target instead of a Pyroblast. **The name carries its own lineage.**

### Druid, Feral 30 / Balance 21

Reaches **Heart of the Wild**, **Wild Aspect** and **Wild Growth**.

**Wildheart.**

Three talents with "wild" in the name and Heart of the Wild as the anchor. Vanilla named the balance-resto hybrid Dreamstate after its key talent, so naming this one after its cluster follows the same instinct. **It is also the only name here that sounds like something a druid would say.**

### Paladin, Retribution 26 / Holy 25

Reaches **Crusade** and **Hammer of Wrath**, plus **Divine Favor** and **Sanctified Judgement**.

**Crusade/Favor**, or **Hammerdin**.

The slash version is accurate. The portmanteau is what would actually stick, because Shockadin already established the pattern and Hammer of Wrath is the ability you press. **Shockadin was a Holy paladin doing damage; a Hammerdin is a Retribution paladin who kept the Light.**

### Rogue, Assassination 21 / Subtlety 20 / Combat 10

Reaches **Toxicology**, **Envenom**, **Hemorrhage**, **Setup** and **Riposte**.

**Hemo/Envenom.**

Hemo rogue is the vanilla term and this build keeps Hemorrhage. What is new is that it also collects poison talents from all three trees, which is the thing the poison rework made possible. **The name records both halves: the old shape and the reason it now works.**

### Shaman, Elemental 30 / Enhancement 21

Reaches **Lightning Mastery** and **Elemental Fury**, plus **Elemental Weapons**.

**Tempest.**

The slash version, Lightning/Weapons, is unhelpful because both words describe half the class. The build's actual identity comes from the reworked Elemental Weapons: *"Lightning Bolt may be cast without stopping your swing."* **This is a shaman who casts and swings at the same time, which no vanilla build did.** A new shape deserves a new word rather than a borrowed one.

### Hunter, Marksmanship 30 / Survival 21

Reaches **Rapid Killing** and **Trapline**, plus **Counterattack**.

**Trapline.**

The talent name is already the build name. It describes what the hunter is doing, it is one word, and it is the reason the build exists. **When a talent is this central, taking its name is what players do anyway.**

### Priest, Shadow 26 / Discipline 25

Reaches **Silence** and **Darkness**, plus **Divine Spirit** and **Shadow Affinity**.

**Silence/Spirit.**

The weakest of the set and worth saying so. It is accurate and it follows the convention, but it does not describe anything a player would feel. **The build is a shadow priest who still brings the raid buff**, and there may be a better word for that which I have not found.

| Druid | Feral 30 / Balance 21 | **Wildheart** | Dreamstate |
| Paladin | Retribution 26 / Holy 25 | **Hammerdin** | Shockadin |

## 4. One thing worth deciding

**These should not go in the documents.**

Vanilla build names were earned rather than assigned. DS/Ruin exists because players found it, argued about it on forums, and the name stuck through use. **A design document that ships with names attached is telling players what to call something they have not discovered yet**, which is the opposite of how any of these precedents worked.

**The useful version is the reverse: build the talents so the names are available.** SL/Ruin names itself because Siphon Life and Ruin are both real talents at those depths. Trapline names itself because the talent is called Trapline. **If the build is nameable, the naming will happen without help.**

Worth keeping as a check on the design rather than as content. **A build nobody can name in three words is probably a build nobody will remember.**
