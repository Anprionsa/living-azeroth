# Voice

Three registers, each owning named ground. Decided August 2026; binding for all copy on this page.

## 1. The chronicle (world-document prose only)

The narrator of a campaign manual who knows the land. Measured, periodic sentences that carry their clause to the end; the Warcraft II manual is the model. Word choice takes a light patina (accounts, provisions, roads, holds) but grammar stays modern.

- Sample: "Whoever holds Grim Batol holds the pass, and the Wildhammer have long memories in the matter."
- Owns: prose paragraphs in the Living Azeroth sections (0 through 13) and the world-describing lines of that document's masthead.
- Does NOT own: views, captions, UI labels, tier/cost/deployment copy — those stay in the client's data voice even inside the world document.
- Engineering realities (costs, tiers, PTR, server budgets) appear as auditor sentences inline, unmarked. The chronicle yields the sentence and takes the next one back.

## 2. The auditor (talent, class absorption, and simulation documents)

The source documents' own spec voice. Claims backed by named evidence, corrections stated plainly, verdicts earned at paragraph ends rather than sprinkled. Median sentence around seven words in the source; match it — fragments are for landings, not texture.

- Sample: "Nine proposals ship to live. The other 65 are why the fresh realm exists."
- Person: third person impersonal. Second person only for what a player experiences.
- Intensifiers (deliberately, genuinely, actually) only where load-bearing — where deleting one changes the claim.

## 3. The client (data, tooltips, UI)

Vanilla client text: terse, mechanical, exact. Talent tooltips are frozen — never restyled, never paraphrased. UI labels say what they do. Empty states point at the control that fixes them.

## Bans (from the design system, still binding)

No em dashes. No exclamation points. No marketing voice (delightful, seamless, exciting). No first person. No Title Case. No emoji. Sentence case everywhere; uppercase only for tracked interface eyebrows. Game proper nouns exactly as the game spells them.

## AI tells to purge on sight

"Additionally / Moreover / Furthermore", "It's worth noting", "not just X but Y" reversals, triadic flourishes, verdict fragments used as texture, intensifiers as filler, "the result is", "in other words".

The sharpest structural tell is the verbless fragment chain: "Not combat behavior. Not quest hooks. Just work, rest, and downtime." Every sentence carries a verb. A short sentence is allowed to land a paragraph; a chain of clipped fragments posing as sentences is not, and provenance is no defense — the pattern gets rewritten even where the source documents carry it.
